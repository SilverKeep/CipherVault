Lacking Practice?

On-paper options for practicing ciphers either takes time to copy or valuable ink when printing. But there isn't another option, or is there? We have a more elegant and efficient method.

One source includes both Aristocrats and Patristocrat. However, ours also has Hill and Nihilist. Beyond that, it provides a simple, interactive interface allowing you to track your progress.

Benefits
Save Time
Copying on paper is a long, intensive process. Even printing a cipher list takes up valuable ink, and an all-too stressful wait. Digital ciphers help avoid all these problems at once!
Practice Efficiently
With the inherent speed, you can just as quickly cycle through so much more in the same time. Our interface was built to replicate the real format on the test, making it equally effective.
Practice More
Though other online resources exist, only ours contains the rest of the ciphers in an easily-toggleable system. Additionally, we are open to any requests for new ciphers, maybe just for fun!
Potentially Asked Questions
What is this resource?

This is an interactive platform designed to help users practice various types of ciphers for Science Olympiad Codebusters and beyond.

How does this help me practice?

We offer an extensive library of cipher types with an easy-to-use interface, allowing you to track your progress and improve efficiently.

Can I request new features?

Yes! We welcome feedback and feature requests. Your suggestions help us to elevate this platform to its maximum. To submit, please reference the form on the footer.

The purpose of this resource is to provide an easy way to practice ciphers directly, tracking progress as you go! It is completely free.
